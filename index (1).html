I use satellites and fieldwork to study how Himalayan glaciers are changing.

Postdoctoral Fellow at the Interdisciplinary Centre for Water Research (ICWaR), Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore.

Research

My research focuses on glaciers in the north-western Himalaya: how their area and debris cover have changed over decades, how fast the ice moves, and how glacial lakes have formed and grown.

For my Ph.D. at the University of Jammu and the Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (ISRO), I studied glacier dynamics and debris thickness on the Durung-Drung and Pensilungpa glaciers. I combined Landsat time series, Google Earth Engine and DInSAR with electrical resistivity surveys on the ice.

I'm now a Postdoctoral Fellow at IISc Bangalore, working on the Cauvery River Basin Management & Studies project (cCauvery). Before that, I worked on wetland and watershed monitoring projects using geospatial tools.
